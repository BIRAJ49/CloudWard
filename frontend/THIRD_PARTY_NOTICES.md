## ObsidianUI

The frontend includes adapted versions of the ObsidianUI **Dotted Grid** and
**Arrow Fill Button** components.

- Source: https://www.obsidianui.dev/
- License: MIT
- Upstream repository: https://gitlab.com/Atharvsinh-codez/ObsidianUI

The adaptations remove framework-specific utilities, add TypeScript types, and
align motion, accessibility, and colors with the CloudWard operations console.
